💡 The Science Behind the Pomodoro Technique

1. Study for 25 Minutes: Focus completely on a single task without phone or tab notifications.
2. Take a 5-Minute Break: Stretch, drink water, or look away from the screen to recharge.
3. Complete 4 Cycles: After finishing 4 study sessions, reward yourself with an extended 15-minute long break.
4. Automatic Cloud Tracking: Every completed study cycle is automatically stored in your database to update your productivity metrics!
